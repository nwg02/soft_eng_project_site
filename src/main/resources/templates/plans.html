UNDERSTAND YOUR OPTIONS

What do the metal levels mean?

Metal levels generally indicate how costs are shared when you use covered services, not the quality of care. Your actual costs depend on the specific plan and your circumstances.

BRONZE

Lower monthly premiums

Bronze plans often have lower premiums and higher out-of-pocket costs when you need care.

Consider if monthly affordability is a major priority.
SILVER

A middle ground

Silver plans balance premiums with out-of-pocket costs. Eligible shoppers can get extra cost-sharing savings through Silver marketplace plans.

Compare carefully if you may qualify for savings.
GOLD

More costs covered when you get care

Gold plans often have higher monthly premiums but lower costs when you use covered care.

Consider if you expect more frequent care.
